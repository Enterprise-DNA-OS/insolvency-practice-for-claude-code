# Selection evidence: 19 October 2026

Insolvency case management was not a built family in `data/rebuilds.json`. Accounting practice management (Karbon, FYI) and tax correspondence already existed; this build is the insolvency desk inside an accounting or restructuring practice: appointments, statutory steps, creditors, proofs, assets, the case cashbook, remuneration approvals, meetings and dividends.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Turnkey IPS (insolvency case management) | 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 20 |
| Tenement management (Landfolio and others) | 3 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 3 | 18 |
| Aircraft maintenance tracking (Traxxall, Veryon) | 2 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 3 | 17 |

Scores are judgement, not measured demand. [Turnkey's site](https://turnkey-ips.com/) describes IPS as "a cost-effective, end-to-end platform for efficient corporate insolvency management" for Australia, New Zealand and beyond, sold as a subscription with paid add-ons (KYC, Confirmation of Payee, Turnkey Pay, Experian creditor reports) and companion products (Turnkey IP, a creditor portal, and Turnkey Proven for proofs and proxies). It publishes no price; the route is a demo and a quote. Aryza's Australian insolvency product is also quoted privately. The bill score of 3 reflects a per-firm subscription plus add-ons for a practice with several practitioners and managers; no NZ or AU customer invoice was verified, so the page and README name no figure.

Aircraft maintenance tracking was set aside on the bill: Software Advice lists Traxxall from US$1,800 a year and Veryon Tracking from US$1,750 a year. Tenement management had thin search demand and few of our buyers.

The buyer fit is the strength: insolvency work sits inside accounting and restructuring practices in both countries, every file runs to a statutory calendar, and the rules are local. This build carries the Australian and New Zealand steps that have a fixed date and says plainly which ones it does not model.
